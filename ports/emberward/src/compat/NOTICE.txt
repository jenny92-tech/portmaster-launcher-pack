MiniLoong / Godot 4.3 compatibility adapter

Original Emberward adapter source: egl_client_extensions.c (MIT).
The per-draw GPU synchronization workaround follows PortMaster-mlp1:
https://github.com/Utility-Muffin-Research-Kitchen/PortMaster-mlp1/blob/1e751b7d99d7b650a640b9f9f3a8a3644f1069bd/docs/compatibility.md#L194-L232
The package retains the stock Mali userspace and existing Weston compositor.
The adapter is process-local and for serialized, single-window MiniLoong GLES.
Rebuild/source and ELF SHA256 values are in loong-egl-compat.json.
